MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (REVISED V2)

Section 1. Definitions and Scope
1.1 "Services" refers to cloud application development and managed infrastructure provided by Supplier to Customer.
1.2 "Fees" refers to all compensation payable under this Agreement.

Section 2. Compensation & Payment Terms
2.1 Customer shall pay Supplier an annual retainer fee of AED 2,500,000 payable monthly in advance within 15 days of invoice.
2.2 Late payments shall accrue interest at 2.0% per month.

Section 3. Liability and Limitation
3.1 Maximum aggregate liability of Supplier under this Agreement shall be strictly capped at AED 5,000,000.
3.2 In no event shall either party be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

Section 4. Termination
4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing at least 30 days written notice to the other party.
4.2 In the event of a material breach, the non-breaching party may terminate immediately upon written notice if uncured after 30 days.

Section 5. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution
5.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied in the Emirate of Dubai.
