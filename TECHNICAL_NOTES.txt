1. HOW QUOTE VERIFICATION WORKS & WHERE IT COULD FAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our quote verification engine enforces zero-trust citation grounding. When an AI
model generates a candidate quote, the engine strips tabs, newlines, and extra
spaces (normalizeWhitespace). It performs a two-pass match against raw document text:
  • Pass 1: Exact Substring Matching against the normalized document string.
  • Pass 2: Fuzzy Token Sliding Window (Levenshtein similarity > 0.88) to catch
    hyphenated line wraps or extraction artifacts.
Upon verification, true page numbers and character offsets are mapped to enable
interactive yellow highlighting in the PDF/DOCX viewer.

Where it could fail:
  • Extracted text with severe OCR distortion or legacy PDF font encoding errors.
  • Short generic quotes (e.g. "Payment terms") occurring dozens of times across
    a contract without sufficient surrounding section context.


2. HOW LARGE DOCUMENTS ARE HANDLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contracts up to 150+ pages are handled through client-side stream chunking:
  • Overlapping Chunking: Text is split into 500-token chunks with 50-token
    overlaps to preserve clause and paragraph continuity.
  • Multi-Stage Retrieval: BM25 lexical keyword scoring combined with monetary
    pattern boosting (+25 score for currency symbols like $, AED, INR) ensures
    financial and liability clauses rank highest for relevant queries.
  • Memory Optimization: Document chunks are persisted in browser LocalStorage
    and loaded lazily, avoiding context window overflows and high API costs.


3. PART C CHOICE, PROGRESS & HARDEST PART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Option Chosen: Part C — Option 2: Agentic Document Research.
• Rationale: Real-world legal research is inherently multi-step—lawyers scan headers,
  look up specific sections, and cross-reference clauses before forming answers.
• Progress: 100% Fully Implemented. The autonomous agent operates in a tool-calling
  loop using search_document, get_section, and list_clauses with a hard safety cap of
  6 execution rounds and a live visual activity timeline.
• Hardest Part: Building deterministic, multi-round tool-calling in a pure frontend
  browser environment while ensuring tool outputs are correctly parsed and verified
  without causing infinite loops or state sync bugs.
